HashMap Pattern:
Used when we need fast lookup of elements.
Common in problems like Two Sum, Subarray Sum, Longest substring without repeating characters.
Time complexity becomes O(n).

Two Pointer Pattern:
Used in sorted arrays or when shrinking/expanding window.
Example: Container with most water, 3Sum, remove duplicates.

Sliding Window Pattern:
Used for subarray/substring problems.
Maintain a window and adjust based on condition.

Prefix Sum Pattern:
Used when we need range sum quickly.
Store cumulative sums to answer queries in O(1).
